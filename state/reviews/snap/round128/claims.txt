How often do NFIP flood claims get paid? N counties compared (FEMA data) Flood Insurance Data How often do NFIP flood claims get paid? Of 267,368 NFIP flood claims for losses from 2020 on, 83.4% had a recorded payment. In the N counties with 500 or more such claims, the share runs from 52.7% in Terrebonne Parish, LA to 94.4% in Somerset County, NJ. 83.4% Claims with a recorded payment, U.S., losses 2020 on 267,368 NFIP claims counted FEMA OpenFEMA NFIP Claims v3, as of August 31, 2026. A recorded payment is any building, contents or Increased Cost of Compliance payment above $0. Left out: 15,438 records FEMA coded "Claim deleted in error" or "Assigned in error"; counted as claims with no payment, the U.S. share would be 78.8%. FEMA's data does not say whether a claim is still open, so a recent claim with no payment may still be paid.
