Relatively Moderate for coastal flooding and Relatively High for inland (river) flooding, by FEMA's county rating. That compares this county with others; it is not a forecast for your address. Coastal flood risk rating (FEMA) Relatively Moderate Inland flood risk rating (FEMA) Relatively High FEMA's rating weighs modeled loss with social vulnerability and community resilience; it is not a prediction for your home, and it can differ from the loss percentile below. Coastal: FEMA models roughly $1.1 million of flood loss a year (buildings, people and farmland valued in dollars, insured or not; a model, not insurance payouts); a separate FEMA measure, loss per dollar of building value: coastal flood loss here is higher than in about 65% of U.S. counties. Inland: FEMA models roughly $279.8 million of flood loss a year (buildings, people and farmland valued in dollars, insured or not; a model, not insurance payouts); a separate FEMA measure, loss per dollar of building value: inland flood loss here is higher than in about 98% of U.S. counties.
