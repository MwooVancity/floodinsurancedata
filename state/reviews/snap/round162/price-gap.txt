What a premium below full-risk means Except for the Emergency Program discount, statutory discounts gradually phase out through an annual increase cap discount at each renewal date until the policy reaches its full-risk premium. By law, most premium rates may not rise more than 18% a year; the rate for some property, including homes that are not a primary residence and business property, rises 25% a year until that group's average rate reaches the level the law sets (42 U.S.C. §4015(e)). FloodSmart puts the limit at 18 to 25% a year for most policyholders. It usually does not apply to new policies, raising your coverage or lowering your deductible can go past it, and fees and surcharges are added on top. The index shows how many policies are below their full-risk premium, not how fast, or by how much, any one bill will change. It is not a forecast.
