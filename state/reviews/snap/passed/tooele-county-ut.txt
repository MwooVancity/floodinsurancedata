How this is counted What each means: zone AE , zone X , zone VE . What the zone codes mean (44 CFR §64.3): A, AE and A1–A30 are high-risk; AO and AH are high-risk with shallow flooding; A99 is high-risk behind a flood barrier FEMA treats as complete; V, VE and V1–V30 are coastal high-risk, flooded by tides with fast-moving water (velocity); B and X are areas of moderate flood hazard, C and X areas of minimal hazard; D has possible but undetermined flood hazards.
