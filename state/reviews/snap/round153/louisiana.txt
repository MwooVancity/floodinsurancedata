Work out when a new NFIP policy, or added or increased NFIP coverage, starts. Without an exception, the earliest start is 12:01 a.m. local time on the 30th calendar day after the application date, if the insurer receives the form and full amount due within 10 calendar days of that date or they are mailed by certified mail within 4 calendar days; otherwise it is the 30th calendar day after the insurer receives them. With the map exception the same receipt rules apply, but the start is the next calendar day instead of the 30th. The map exception holds only if the insurer receives the form and full amount due within 13 months of the map revision's effective date; after that, the 30-day wait applies. With the loan exception, coverage starts at the time of the loan closing if the insurer receives the form and full amount due within 30 calendar days of closing when the lender, title company or settlement attorney pays, or within 10 calendar days when you or anyone else pays; otherwise the earliest start is the 30th calendar day after the insurer receives them. Contents-only coverage qualifies only if the contents are part of the security for the loan.
