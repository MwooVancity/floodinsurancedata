
# Zone pages (new type), reviewed after the page sections

## flood-zones/ae
- **MAJOR** Hero base. The page is headed "Flood zone AE" and the big figure is "$1,148 / Median / yr, all A zones (AE, A, AH, AO and others)", but every row of the state table is the exact-AE median (FL $1,305, TX $1,234, LA $1,247, NJ $1,324) and the unit count beside it is "2,207,390 NFIP policy units rated AE". A reader with an AE letter gets a hero number for a different population than the page title and the table, and it sits below the AE rows of the largest states. The label is truthful but small; the number answers "all A zones", not "AE". Show the AE-only national median, or say "not AE only" beside the number.
- **MINOR** The VE page explains "Most insured units in this zone are not in the median: 148,995 units ... 18,640 single-family primary-home policies". AE (2,207,390 units vs 1,253,870 policies) and X (1,650,846 vs 1,078,391) have the same gap but no such note. Inconsistent.
- **MINOR** "Outside them, a lender can still require it": "them" is unclear (outside what?). Title "who must insure" reads oddly.
- **GAP** Top results (Kin, SmartFinancial, insure.com style) give a cost-driver list (elevation, base flood elevation, elevation certificate as a way to pay less) and a zone comparison table. We print no "can I pay less" line. The first Google results for "flood zone AE insurance cost" are mostly spam clones with conflicting ranges ($564-$7,500, $934), so a sourced median plus range already beats them.
- 30-second test: PASS. Median, range, "required" answer and the FEMA map link are on screen 1.
Verdict: PASS

## flood-zones/x
- **MAJOR** Unlabelled base in the table. Column header "Claim dollars outside A and V" shows percentages (Texas 47.2%, North Dakota 62.9%) with no "share of" and no "in this state". Next to "Units rated X" and "Median zone X", a reader takes it as an X-zone claims figure. It is the share of all state claim dollars on claims in any zone other than A or V (not only X). The explaining paragraph is after the table. The header must say "Share of the state's claim dollars on claims outside A and V".
- **MINOR** Hero says "NFIP policy units rated X" (1,650,846) but the median is for "X, B and C zones". Labelled, but a reader will see two zone sets.
- **MINOR** Florida and New Jersey both show 8.1%. I did not recompute; confirm it is not a copy error.
- **GAP** Top answers to "flood zone X insurance required" say first "not federally required, but lenders may require it, and Zone X is not risk-free". Our "Am I required" paragraph answers the same but the sentence "Outside them, a lender can still require it" is weak. Also no "is it worth it" line, which is a decision aid we cannot print as advice.
- 30-second test: PASS for "required"; "Does zone X flood? Yes." is a strong answer, but it sits below a 50-row table.
Verdict: FAIL
